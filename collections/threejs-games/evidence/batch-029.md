- Rendering: The actual index loads the vendored Three library and game scripts. main.js directly creates Three WebGLRenderer and drives the Hockey scene with requestAnimationFrame; hockey.js renders the 3D table, mallets and pucks while project-authored helpers connect Box2D physics and the AI opponent. [Renderer source](https://github.com/MortimerGoro/AirHockeyWebGL/blob/9ad9bf421c2787a0b4a4cca2e7c12772f099ff68/src/main.js#L25).
