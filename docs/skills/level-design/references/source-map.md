# Source Map and Reading Coverage

## Snapshot and scope

- Source: [The Level Design Book](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/).
- Reading snapshot: **2026-10-16**.
- Discovery: [llms.txt](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/llms.txt), checked against [sitemap-pages.xml](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/sitemap-pages.xml).
- **94 unique indexed pages retrieved and read.** The sitemap and documentation index matched after normalizing the home page and Markdown suffixes.
- Reading scope: the page bodies, tables, figure captions/alt text, examples, and in-page technical listings. Image/video embeds were not a substitute for watching the media or playing the games; figures were not exhaustively visually inspected.
- External linked articles, talks, books, source repositories, asset libraries, and games are **not** counted as independently read, watched, audited, or played.
- Temporary source caches are not included in this skill. This directory contains new wording, condensed analysis, task routing, checklists, and templates rather than the source corpus.

## How to interpret coverage

Each listed page was read. “Body” marks pages with substantive text. The entire book is under construction, and some substantive pages also contain TODOs.

“Draft,” “short,” “outline,” and “STUB” describe the snapshot, not a judgment of importance. Where this skill extends a draft into practical checks, it identifies those checks as adaptations rather than attributing missing material to the original author.

The book's authorial interpretations and forecasts are kept separate from established facts and measured results. Numerical examples, tool versions, platform opinions, prices, and community descriptions require current verification when relevant.

The filled fields, small exercises, and diagnostic examples added to this adaptation are GameMap practice material. They do not expand the historical 94-page inventory, complete the source's unfinished chapters, or report newly executed playtests. Sample events are labeled hypothetical; measurements and results must come from the reader's own project. The historical reading snapshot above remains unchanged.

The brush-map exercise also uses the [TrenchBroom manual](https://trenchbroom.github.io/manual/latest/) and the ericw-tools maintainer references for [qbsp](https://ericwa.github.io/ericw-tools/doc/qbsp.html), [vis](https://ericwa.github.io/ericw-tools/doc/vis.html), and [light](https://ericwa.github.io/ericw-tools/doc/light.html). The TrenchBroom `/latest/` URL moves with releases; record the installed editor and compiler versions when using the exercise.

## Adaptation boundaries

- **Entry point:** SKILL.md contains task routing and a minimal evidence-driven operating loop.
- **Twelve topical references:** consolidate related pages while preserving important disagreements, genre assumptions, and source gaps.
- **Three templates:** newly organized brief, playtest report, and review structures. They are not original forms from the book.
- **No hidden source expansion:** a title-only case study does not become a completed analysis; an external bibliography entry does not become inherited evidence.
- **No bundled third-party assets or archival code:** especially the separately licensed MDL material.
- **Rights and attribution:** see [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md). Adapted explanatory material is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## Refresh procedure

1. Retrieve the index and sitemap again.
2. Compare additions, removals, renamed URLs, and substantive page changes.
3. Read changed pages, including their rights notices and newly completed sections.
4. Update relevant topical references, source-state labels, and the snapshot.
5. Verify internal links, source coverage, frontmatter, and task routing.
6. Keep adaptations distinguishable from source claims and actual test evidence.
