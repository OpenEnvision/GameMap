Blender

An open-source 3D creation suite for modeling, materials, rigging, animation, simulation, and rendering.

Why it belongs here

Primarily supports game asset and scene production, with agent-generated Python scripts for repeatable tasks. Blender is not a game runtime engine; verify scale, coordinates, materials, rigs, and animation after importing into Unity, UE, Godot, or a browser.

Katana Kitsune: Prototype

Choose a kitsune fighter and face yokai in an isometric action roguelike with katana combat, stamina, pickups and successive dungeon floors. The silent prototype supports local multiplayer, with additional party systems documented in the current source.

Polar Icecapade

Slide across icy 3D arenas, use a three-hit melee combo to propel yourself forward, and fire projectiles that push you backward. Open gates and collect coins while confronting a rat regime in a short game-jam adventure.

Game example

Core Business

Drill for minerals, manage fuel and hull condition, and spend earnings at surface shops in a non-commercial mining tech demo inspired by Motherload. Three.js renders the 2.5D world while Blender scripts produce its 3D assets.

Game example

Golden Hour Skatepark

Skate an original beachside park: carve transitions, ollie, flip, grind, manual, bail and recover. This complete third-person sandbox has transient trick combos and a changeable time of day, with no levels or persistent score.

Game example

Sprout Quest

Explore a small world, fight real-time arena battles, gather materials, craft gear and rebuild a village. Sprout Quest is a mobile-first adventure with a 2D world and live-rendered 3D characters and items.
