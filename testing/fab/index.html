AI use and evaluation boundary

Check the listing license and AI-use designation before testing with AI or sharing a dataset. A listing field is not a catalog-wide rule; free pricing is not an open license.

Setup

  1. Record the exact listing, acquired version, license and AI-use fields.
  2. Import only after the intended automated use and distribution are covered.

Limitations

The Standard license does not generally permit standalone asset redistribution. The official summary says Reference-Only access does not include source-format files, so confirm the acquired tier before planning export or import fixtures. Engine-specific content and individual terms require separate review; no local import was performed.
