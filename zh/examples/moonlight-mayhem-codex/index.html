研究这个工作流

  1. Play the heist and trace crew rescue, security detection, interactions, and escape.
  2. Read the creator’s build account and compare the original brief with the result.
  3. Follow the transcript and commit history to study scene construction, generated art, and the visual bug fix.
  4. Serve the complete repository locally, then study one mechanic and its supporting assets.
