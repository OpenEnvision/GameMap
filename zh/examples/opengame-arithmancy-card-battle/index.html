AI 工作流与开发背景

研究作者的知识型战斗提示词如何变成问答、卡牌、回合和连击系统，再通过小范围规则修改观察代理如何维护相互关联的状态。

研究这个工作流

  1. Play a duel with the mouse and observe how answers affect spells and battle progress.
  2. Compare the original prompt with the playable quiz, card selection, and combo feedback.
  3. Inspect the exact archive’s quiz, card, turn, and combo systems before changing a rule.
  4. Ask an agent to revise one question set or scoring rule, then test both correct and incorrect answers.
