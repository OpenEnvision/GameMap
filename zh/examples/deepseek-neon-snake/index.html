研究这个工作流

  1. Play a round and observe how eating food advances the level.
  2. Read the repository’s DeepSeek attribution and gameplay instructions.
  3. Inspect the game loop, input queue, food spawning, and collision handling.
  4. Change one level’s parameters and verify start, pause, loss, and restart.
