研究这个工作流

  1. Flip two cards and watch how matches and mismatches behave.
  2. Compare the exact prompt with the complete inline implementation.
  3. Inspect shuffle, turn locking and reset during a delayed mismatch.
  4. Change the deck and complete a round, then verify reset and keyboard access.
