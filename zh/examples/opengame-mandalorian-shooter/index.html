AI 工作流与开发背景

作者将其作为 OpenGame 生成案例发布。可以比较战斗与移动需求及实际实现，再研究生成的关卡和玩法场景。

研究这个工作流

  1. Play the live build and test movement, aiming, shooting, and the jetpack dash.
  2. Read the original prompt and identify the requested combat, cover, and rescue mechanics.
  3. Inspect the exact project archive and follow the Imperial Base level’s gameplay state.
  4. Revise one mechanic with an agent, then check collisions, damage, and objective progression in play.
