AI 工作流与开发背景

作者将作品集归属于 Cursor AI，并说明这款竞技场游戏是孩子的 AI 辅助创作，没有详细提示词历史。

研究这个工作流

  1. Try the arena and compare single-player and local multiplayer behavior.
  2. Read the creator’s AI contribution statement.
  3. Inspect demoderby/game.js to follow car movement, damage, and opponent behavior.
  4. Tune one vehicle parameter and test collisions with both players.
