AI 工作流与开发背景

作者将作品集归属于 Gemini 与 Claude 的迭代开发，并说明做过少量手工平衡调整；此游戏没有独立提示词日志。

研究这个工作流

  1. Begin a hunt and test movement, flashlight, sprinting, and pumpkin interaction.
  2. Read the author’s AI contribution disclosure.
  3. Study procedural scenery, the stalker state, and its line-of-sight behavior.
  4. Serve the HTML locally and adjust one visibility or stamina parameter.
