代码许可

Not a software project

资产许可

Per-entry licenses, including CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA and GPL variants

AI 使用与评估条件

There is no catalog-wide AI authorization. Select files under a suitable license and assess their attribution, share-alike and any additional rights before publishing a dataset.

可以尝试的任务

  • Import a tilemap and validate tile sizes, transparency and collision alignment.
  • Generate an attribution manifest from the exact selected entries.

配置步骤

  1. Filter by the license required for the intended test and distribution.
  2. Archive the selected entry URL, author, license text and original files.

使用限制

Free downloads have different licenses and asset quality. The FAQ says preview images and audio can have different rights from downloadable assets; do not treat those previews as licensed test-fixture files unless explicitly covered. No individual asset was locally imported or cleared as part of this catalog review.
