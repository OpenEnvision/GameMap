使用说明
Python, pretrained checkpoints and a compatible CUDA GPU are required; Atari and CS:GO use different documented setups.

实际使用注意事项

  • MIT-licensed source and pretrained playable Atari/CS:GO world models are linked. Local interaction needs Python/model dependencies and a CUDA GPU; the project reports approximately 10 FPS on an RTX 3090 for the CS:GO setup. The external hosted link was reachable, but its execution was not independently playtested.
  • A repository mentioning this paper was found through paper metadata, but author ownership or an official project-to-code link was not established. It is not counted as official source code.
  • A project-linked demo endpoint was found, but a working interactive application was not established.
