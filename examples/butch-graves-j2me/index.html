WollyMonjac credits Claude Opus 5.5 with code, levels, art and audio tools. The human supplied the brief and direction, played builds and reported problems; the creator also explains simulator, emulator and real-device testing separately.

Study this workflow

  1. Download the full or lite JAR and open it in a compatible J2ME emulator or MIDP 2.0 phone.
  2. Try moving, turning, firing, using doors and switching weapons during the opening episode.
  3. Read the creator’s implementation account and compare src/E.java with the generated resource packs.
  4. Change one level or resource, follow DEVELOPER.md to rebuild, and verify the phone API, memory and audio constraints on the target runtime.
