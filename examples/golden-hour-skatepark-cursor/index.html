Game example

Golden Hour Skatepark

Skate an original beachside park: carve transitions, ollie, flip, grind, manual, bail and recover. This complete third-person sandbox has transient trick combos and a changeable time of day, with no levels or persistent score.

AI workflow / foundation context

The author says Cursor AI agents implemented the game through builder and visual-critic passes, with human playtests refining controls. Code-built environments and procedural riding are combined with Cursor-generated artwork, credited CC0 audio/textures and Mixamo recovery animations.

Study this workflow

  1. Play the park and test pushing, carving, one trick, a bail and recovery.
  2. Read the making-of account and separate human feedback, agent implementation and third-party assets.
  3. Use Node 22 with pnpm 10.18.0, then pnpm install and pnpm dev; inspect the collision mesh and character pipeline.
  4. Ask an agent to adjust one movement or camera parameter, then compare transition riding, tricks and recovery across the park.

  • Play Golden Hour Skatepark

    Browser skating sandbox with keyboard, optional mouse/gamepad and touch controls. Press any key, click or tap to take over from the title-screen rider. Phone testing reported by the creator used desktop emulation; Firefox and desktop Safari remain untested.
