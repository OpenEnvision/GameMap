Arkanoid Neon

Study this workflow

  1. Play a stage and compare paddle control, collision behavior, power-ups and losing a life.
  2. Read the creator’s original Swedish request and AI attribution.
  3. Open index.html locally and trace the game loop, level data and procedural graphics in game.js.
  4. Ask an agent to change one brick pattern or power-up, then test launching, losing a life and restarting.
