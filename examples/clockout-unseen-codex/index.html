Clock Out Unseen

Sneak out of three offices before time expires, using furniture, coffee distractions and a temporary folder disguise to avoid patrol sightlines and reach an elevator.

AI workflow / foundation context

Ryan-fm supplied the office-stealth concept and approved the visual direction. GPT-6 Astra in Codex iterated on collisions, sightlines, patrols, interactions, progression, mobile controls and tests. A separate image-generation tool created office and character art.

Study this workflow

  1. Start Normal mode and move behind office furniture.
  2. Read the selected prompts and model contribution record.
  3. Trace movement, line-of-sight and disguise rules in the engine.
  4. Change one patrol rule and check immediate detection, retry and level progression.
