AI workflow / foundation context

This author-published OpenGame case connects a detailed action-game prompt to a live result and its generated source. Assess the implementation against the brief instead of treating the prompt as proof that every feature works.

Study this workflow

  1. Play the browser build and compare the selectable heroes and their combat feedback.
  2. Read the original brief and turn its movement, skills, stages, and boss requirements into a short checklist.
  3. Open the exact source archive and follow character selection into the three level scenes.
  4. Choose one requirement to revise with an agent, then replay the relevant stage and check for regressions.
